About me

Experienced, thoughtful teaching for adults who want substance, not fluff.

I hold a double major bachelor’s degree in Linguistics and German Studies, a minor in Spanish, and a Master’s degree in German Studies. My graduate training also included Arizona teaching certification, and I was recognized by the Dean of Humanities as an Outstanding Graduate Assistant in Teaching.

I taught German at the university level from 101 through 202, spent a semester teaching at the high school level, and taught intensive intermediate German for American study abroad students at the University of Leipzig for two summers.

Today I teach adults in Berlin and online, with lessons shaped by real learner goals, strong explanation, and a calm, human approach.
